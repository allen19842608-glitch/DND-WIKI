# Appendix B: Monster Lists

## Monsters by Environment

The following tables organize monsters by environment and challenge rating. These tables omit monsters that don't customarily inhabit the environments included here, such as angels and demons.

- {@filter Arctic monsters|bestiary|environment=arctic}

- {@filter Coastal monsters|bestiary|environment=coastal}

- {@filter Desert monsters|bestiary|environment=desert}

- {@filter Forest monsters|bestiary|environment=forest}

- {@filter Grassland monsters|bestiary|environment=grassland}
