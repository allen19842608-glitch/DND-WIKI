# Chapter 3: Character Classes

Character class provides a character's most exciting capabilities. This chapter offers twelve classes, each of which contains four subclasses—all summarized below.

## {@class Barbarian|XPHB}

Storm with Rage, and wade into hand-to-hand combat. Then follow the Path of the...

- {@class Barbarian|XPHB|Path of the Berserker|Berserker|XPHB} to unleash raw violence.

- {@class Barbarian|XPHB|Path of the Wild Heart|Wild Heart|XPHB} to manifest kinship with animals.

- {@class Barbarian|XPHB|Path of the World Tree|World Tree|XPHB} to tap into cosmic vitality.

- {@class Barbarian|XPHB|Path of the Zealot|Zealot|XPHB} to rage in union with a god.

## {@class Bard|XPHB}

Perform spells that inspire and heal allies or beguile foes. Then join the College of...

- {@class Bard|XPHB|College of Dance|Dance|XPHB} to harness agility in battle.

- {@class Bard|XPHB|College of Glamour|Glamour|XPHB} to weave beguiling Feywild magic.

- {@class Bard|XPHB|College of Lore|Lore|XPHB} to collect knowledge and magical secrets.

- {@class Bard|XPHB|College of Valor|Valor|XPHB} to wield weapons with spells.

## {@class Cleric|XPHB}

Invoke divine magic to heal, bolster, and smite. Then harness the...

- {@class Cleric|XPHB|Life Domain|Life|XPHB} to be a master of healing.

- {@class Cleric|XPHB|Light Domain|Light|XPHB} to wield searing, warding light.

- {@class Cleric|XPHB|Trickery Domain|Trickery|XPHB} to bedevil foes with mischief.

- {@class Cleric|XPHB|War Domain|War|XPHB} to inspire valor and chastise foes.

## {@class Druid|XPHB}

- {@class Druid|XPHB|Circle of the Sea|Sea|XPHB} to channel tides and storms.

- {@class Fighter|XPHB|Battle Master|Battle Master|XPHB} to use special combat maneuvers.

- {@class Fighter|XPHB|Champion|Champion|XPHB} to strive for peak combat prowess.

- {@class Monk|XPHB|Warrior of Mercy|Mercy|XPHB} to heal or harm with a touch.

- {@class Paladin|XPHB|Oath of Devotion|Devotion|XPHB} to emulate the angels of justice.

- {@class Ranger|XPHB|Beast Master|Beast Master|XPHB} to bond with a primal beast.
