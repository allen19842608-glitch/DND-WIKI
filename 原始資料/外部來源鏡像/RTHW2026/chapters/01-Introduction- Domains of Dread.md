# Introduction: Domains of Dread

The Mists reach from the darkest shadows of the multiverse, condemning the wicked and the brave to endless nightmares as prisoners of the Domains of Dread. These grim netherworlds compose the Ravenloft setting and are horrific backdrops for Dungeons & Dragons adventures steeped in fear. In these haunted realms, adventurers might oppose villainous Darklords, seek escape from the Mists, or experience any terrifying adventure you can imagine.

## Using This Book

This book explores the Domains of Dread and helps you create frightful adventures and heroes ready to confront horrors. It requires the {@book Player's Handbook|XPHB}, {@book Dungeon Master's Guide|XDMG}, and {@book Monster Manual|XMM}.

## The Land of the Mists

This section provides you, the DM, an overview of Ravenloft. For details on specific Domains of Dread, see {@book chapter 2|RHW|2}.

### Nightmare Logic

Ravenloft cultivates dread in defiance of logic. Domains don't abut one another, historians don't record coherent pasts, and communities cloak ignorance in strange traditions. The Domains of Dread operate on the same logic as nightmares: Whatever is the most terrifying is true. The Dark Powers, the Mists, and the nature of domains are purposefully mysterious. Mundane details and the lives of domain inhabitants are constructed on illusions and impossibilities. Should characters realize the surreality of the setting, those revelations only create a stronger sense of existential dread and suggest greater questions. Ultimately, none can ever fully understand the Domains of Dread.

### The Dark Powers

The Dark Powers are faceless, godlike forces that manipulate reality within the Domains of Dread. Few even suspect their existence. These enigmatic forces help you create horror adventures and serve two complementary roles:

- Narrative

- Practical

### The Mists

The Mists of Ravenloft appear without warning and move with eerie intention. Areas within the Mists are {@variantrule Heavily Obscured|XPHB}. No amount of wind can disperse the Mists.

The Mists serve the following roles:

- Dark Clutches

- Domain Borders

- Transportation

#### Mysteries of the Mists

The inhabitants of the Domains of Dread ascribe sinister stories to the Mists but accept the Mists as a natural part of their homeland. Those who travel between domains refer to their world as the Land of the Mists. Any supernatural happening or malicious force might be blamed on the Mists. Those who interact with the Mists—or who come from beyond—are typically met with suspicion.

#### Mist Talismans

Mist talismans are nonmagical objects that resonate with certain domains. They might be any object, and some might be aligned with multiple domains. By holding or wearing a Mist talisman and focusing on the domain it's associated with, a creature in the Mists can reach the intended domain after {@dice 1d6} hours of travel. Creatures following within 30 feet of a Mist talisman's bearer reach the same destination. Mist talismans associated with domains are detailed in {@book chapter 2|RHW|2}.

Mist talismans don't allow their bearers to escape domains with closed borders (see "{@area Darklords|00b|x}"). Similarly, if the borders of the destination domain are closed, roll on the Wandering the Mists table to determine what happens.

#### Wandering the Mists

So long as a Darklord hasn't closed their domain's borders, creatures that enter the Mists might travel to other domains. When characters without a Mist talisman enter the Mists surrounding a domain, they emerge elsewhere after {@dice 1d6} hours of travel. Roll on or take inspiration from the Wandering the Mists table to determine where the Mists relocate characters.

### Domains

The Domains of Dread are untold numbers of individual demiplanes hidden in the Shadowfell. Some are created entirely by the {@area Dark Powers|005|x}, while others the Mists claimed from elsewhere in the multiverse and bear evidence of other worlds. Domains might change as the Dark Powers find new ways to torment their captives, with domains changing shape, merging, fading away, reappearing, and even repeating events.

The Domains of Dread share these features:

- Domains Are Finite

- Domains Are Isolated

- Domains Hold Darklords

{@book Chapter 2|RHW|2} describes some of the most notorious Domains of Dread, while {@book chapter 3|RHW|3} explores how to use domains in horror adventures. Use the methods in {@book chapter 4|RHW|4} to create domains of your own.

### Darklords

Every domain torments a Darklord, a villain whose depravity attracted the attention of the {@area Dark Powers|005|x} and led to their imprisonment in the {@area Domains of Dread|00a|x}. A Darklord might be anyone in a domain, from a national leader to an unassuming resident. Darklords share these qualities:

- Darklords Are Evil

- Darklords Are Prisoners

- Darklords Are Tormented

- Darklords Influence Domains

- Darklords Are Active

- Darklords Don't Realize They're Darklords

- Closing Domain Borders

Chapter 2 presents stat blocks for numerous Darklords. {@book Chapter 3|RHW|3} explores how to use Darklords in your horror adventures, while {@book chapter 4|RHW|4} aids in creating your own Darklords.

### People of the Mists

The inhabitants of the {@area Domains of Dread|00a|x} fall into these categories:

- Soulless Shells

- Bright Souls

- Strangers

#### Death and Rebirth

The spirits of those who die in the Domains of Dread or the Mists don't travel to the afterlife. Those magically returned to life after being dead for at least 24 hours return with the grim realization that they're trapped within the Mists.

The souls of those who die and aren't returned to life are trapped within the Domains of Dread. The Dark Powers might eventually reincarnate these souls. Individuals who inherit a soul often look like their predecessor and might recall memories of their past lives.

### Magic in the Mists

Some magic functions differently in the {@area Domains of Dread|00a|x}. Characters who receive spells from otherworldly forces or patrons do so as normal, but the magic noted here functions in distinct ways:

- Banishment

- Escape

- Etherealness

- Extradimensional Spaces

- Otherworldly Contact

- Planar Boundaries

- Planar Travel

- Summoning

#### Cosmetic Magic Effects

Magic effects produced in Ravenloft appear more sinister than they do elsewhere in the multiverse. For example, a creature conjured by {@spell Find Familiar|XPHB} or {@spell Find Steed|XPHB} might appear unnatural or deathly, or the {@spell Find the Path|XPHB} spell might cause a silent, childlike spirit to guide the caster to their destination. Describe such effects in whatever way reinforces a horrific atmosphere, drawing inspiration from the domain where your adventure takes place.

### Life in the Mists

The {@area Domains of Dread|00a|x} might seem surreal to individuals from other worlds, but for the residents of the {@area Mists|006|x}, Ravenloft's bizarre qualities are normal parts of reality.

#### Shared Delusion

Domain residents don't see incongruities in their existence. Societal pressure and soulless individuals controlled by the {@area Dark Powers|005|x} encourage inhabitants to embrace ignorance, dismiss coincidences, or doubt their senses. Residents of the Domains of Dread are no less perceptive than people elsewhere in the multiverse, but their home domains manipulate them into believing everything is as it should be, must be, and always was.

Those who call domain residents' reality into question are likely to be dismissed, distrusted, or worse. Individuals who speak of other worlds are broadly considered to be liars or delusional. Few individuals are open-minded to such concepts.

#### Adventurers

Ravenloft rarely gives rise to heroes. For the inhabitants of most domains, survival is the only act of courage they pursue. Those willing to confront the terrors of the {@area Mists|006|x} are typically strangers from other worlds or domain residents jarred from their apathy by traumatic events.

Adventurers find few peers in the Land of the Mists. Guilds of adventurers and shops catering to their needs generally don't exist. As a result, the challenge of confronting evil rests squarely with a few rare souls. {@book Chapter 3|RHW|3} provides details on wanderers who might aid adventurers across domains.

#### Culture and Technology

Each domain has a distinct culture. Such cultures might be inspired by societies elsewhere in the multiverse or fantasies manufactured to torment a {@area Darklord|00b|x}. Traditions are purportedly based on history, but few domains have coherent records of the past. Details of ages before a Darklord's arrival are usually vague or absent.

Similarly, every domain has its own level of technological advancement, the details of which are up to you. In some domains, firearms and clockwork devices are common, while others might feature cutting-edge weird science.

No cultural exchange occurs between the {@area Domains of Dread|00a|x}. While evidence of one domain might appear in another, such things are viewed with suspicion.

#### Currency

Some domains mint their own gold, silver, and copper coins. Though these currencies bear different markings, merchants aren't particular about the designs stamped on their currency. Platinum and electrum coins rarely circulate through the domains, but they appear often enough—originating from hidden troves or ancient vaults—that no trader questions their value.

#### Language

Common is the shared language of the Domains of Dread. Some domains might have unique words, and speakers from different domains might have distinctive accents, but individuals from different domains are understandable to one another. Other {@table Choose Languages; Standard Languages|XPHB|languages} from the {@book Player's Handbook|XPHB} are also known among the domains, but their rarity varies between domains as you see fit.

#### Religion

In many domains, residents worship stern but vague gods, and clergy have scant supernatural powers. Any deity, patron, or philosophy from anywhere in the multiverse might have followers in the {@area Domains of Dread|00a|x}, though these followers' beliefs might be unconventional. The {@area Dark Powers|005|x} also breathe power into the faith of delusional zealots. Whether otherworldly forces or the Dark Powers empower faithful spellcasters is up to you.

##### Ezra

Mysteriously, worship of one god has its origins in the {@area Mists|006|x}. The god Ezra is depicted as a vaporous figure with dark, billowing hair and is believed to be able to manipulate the Mists. Ezra's holy symbol is a sprig of belladonna atop a silver kite shield. Ezra's followers across domains view the deity in contradictory ways. For some, Ezra is a stalwart guardian, while others perceive the god as a soul-stealing embodiment of the Mists. The truths of Ezra's existence and nature are mysteries.

#### Time and Dates

The Domains of Dread don't share a calendar. However, in most domains, locals measure time by "moons" rather than months. Each moon begins on the first night of a full moon and lasts a full lunar cycle. A year consists of twelve moons.

Although domains don't subscribe to a shared history, the populace of all domains inexplicably accept the current year as 735. Some local histories cleave to anomalous dates and methods of tracking years, but these are considered obsolete. No such irregularities exist in {@book Barovia|RHW|2}, though, where year 1 corresponds with the founding of Barovia by the von Zarovich family.

#### Travel

Most domain residents have little interest in domains beyond their own, and the nature of the Mists makes mercantile ventures and military conquests between domains essentially impossible. Nevertheless, rare individuals travel between the domains either at random, using {@area Mist talismans|008|x} (see "{@area The Mists|006|x}"), or by engaging mist-wandering guides. Those who enter the Mists by road, river, or sea usually emerge in a new domain by the same method. This means that domains with no coastline must be reached by methods other than ship, but also that travelers following roads rarely emerge from the Mists on the sea—though they might arrive inland within a domain surrounded by water.
